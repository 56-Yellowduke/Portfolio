Who am I?

I'm a Procurement Data Consultant & UI/UX Designer, building toward AI/ML and backend development. I bring a systems-thinker's eye to both data and design. On the data side, I restructure procurement and inventory systems, turning messy records into clean, usable structure. On the design side, I've designed apps like Plan Ahead and led a redesign project for Opay — work built in Figma with a focus on clean, intuitive, user-centered experiences. I'm self-taught in Python (OOP, data fundamentals, real projects on GitHub), HackerRank-certified, and always building. I believe good systems — and good design — solve real problems.
