    Plan Ahead

    You show up. We handle the rest.

    Plan Ahead is a marketplace that connects event owners, called Hosts, with event planners. A Host browses planners and their past work, books and pays inside the app, and the planner handles everything so the Host only has to turn up as a guest.

    It covers every kind of event, from birthdays to corporate events and burials. One app, two account types: Host and planner. I'm building it as a clickable, mobile-style web app, with real sign-up and saved bookings and chats next.

    1. Browse Pick a category such as birthday and see event photos from different planners, with prices. Planners near you show first.
    2. Check the work Open an event to see more photos, a description of how it went, and reviews.
    3. Talk it through Discuss details in the in-app chat before you book, so there's a record if anything is disputed.
    4. Book and pay The app holds your payment. Part goes to the planner upfront as a deposit, the rest is released after you confirm the event happened.
    Planners are verified
    An ID-verified badge, reviews only from real bookings, stats like events completed and response time, and photos tied to actual bookings.
    No contact details in chat
    Sharing phone numbers or other contact details is prohibited. Offenders get a strike.
    Blocking has a cooling-off period
    You can't block a profile for at least three months after finishing a deal. Reporting is always available, and the app can block for confirmed harassment.
    One cancellation policy
    A single policy for everyone, so it doesn't change from planner to planner.
